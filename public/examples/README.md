# Bundled example music and audio

- **160 Eight-Measure Exercises, Op. 821 No. 1**, Carl Czerny. Piano MIDI from the [Mutopia Project](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=2060), typeset by Manuel Castejon Limas; listed there as public domain. The app imports a quantized MIDI transcription, not the source engraving.
- **Greensleaves**, traditional. Guitar MIDI from the [Mutopia Project](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=109), maintained by Aaron Fontaine; listed there as public domain. The app assigns the acoustic nylon guitar sound to its MIDI transcription; it does not infer guitar fingering or tablature.
- **Drum Kit Groove** is an original example for this app, using General MIDI bass drum 36, snare 38, and closed hi-hat 42.

Playback uses GeneralUser GS v1.471 by S. Christian Collins, distributed under the terms in `public/soundfont/GeneralUser-LICENSE.txt`. Its author permits use in software and music, and notes uncertainty about the origins of some older samples. This approximately 31 MB bank is loaded when a score player starts, and is larger than the previous 1.4 MB bank; device and network performance will vary. The original Sonivox bank remains in the repository.

Chopin piano examples, typeset by Magnus Lewis-Smith from Edition Peters (1879), are listed as public domain by Mutopia:

- **Prelude in E minor, Op. 28 No. 4**: [source and license](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=468).
- **Raindrop Prelude, Op. 28 No. 15**: [source and license](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=471).
- **Prelude in C minor, Op. 28 No. 20**: [source and license](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=472).

These are complete MIDI transcriptions quantized to sixteenths, not reproductions of the source engraving. MIDI does not preserve all printed expression, fingering, layout, or pedal markings. Both hands are combined into one piano grand staff without removing notes, and the original MIDI is retained with each editable copy.
